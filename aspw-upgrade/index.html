Questions people ask before booking

I'm a complete beginner. Is this for me?

Yes. The workshop starts from how a digital camera works and builds up from there. You don't need to know any jargon before you arrive.

I've been shooting for years. Will I learn anything?

Most likely. Many students who join have used their camera for years but taught themselves in pieces. The workshop puts those pieces into one structure and fills the gaps you didn't know you had.

Which camera do I need?

A DSLR or mirrorless camera gets the most out of the two days. If you have a compact camera with manual controls, the concepts apply to it too. Confirm the compact-camera answer.

Is it in person or online?

In person, in the Toehold classroom in Jayanagar, Bengaluru, with the instructor in the room for both days.
